## 2. What converts (every pattern tied to a Meeting Booked / Prospect Verified on tape)

1. **Discovery question tied to a specific gap BEFORE proof.** "Are you able to achieve the
   registrations that you want?" → prospect names the real pain: "not always... looking at the
   job titles... CSOs or director-plus, that is our ICP to get" → meeting (Semgrep, 374832207).
2. **Case study as curiosity hook, not stat recitation.** Framed as something to SHOW that
   cannot travel by email — "the case study includes the ICP data we used, I can't put that in
   an email" — converted a triple email-deflection into a meeting (Quantexa call 371898704).
   Used ONCE in 56 calls. The same AWS reference recited as numbers with no tie to the
   prospect's situation was flat or harmful every time.
3. **Accept the prospect's precondition, deliver a concrete artifact fast, lock logistics
   on-call.** "I'm not wanting to commit to meetings until I see audience alignment first" →
   committed to a data count by next day → conditional meeting booked same call (Benepass,
   374861336). Same shape: service summary emailed within 30 min + placeholder invite (LeverX,
   378206266). Saylee's 7 bookings almost all follow this pattern.
4. **Low-stakes reframe on hierarchy/authority anxiety.** "I was not looking to make any kind
   of changes... more like an introductory call" → the prospect proposed the meeting date
   herself (PwC Finland, 378169083; also Alvys, 378613943: "not asking you to buy anything").
5. **Let the prospect define success criteria and engage on their terms.** "My concern is
   outcome — can you fix 10 meetings in a month" (Oleria, 377720687). Staying in that
   conversation, not re-pitching, produced the positive disposition.
6. **Hold the ask against email-deflection — with a reason.** The only sustained-email-push
   call that still booked: SDR said plainly that email alone won't give them what they need,
   named a tentative calendar date instead (milestones.com, 378836383).
7. **Role-specific personalization from their own profile/remit.** "I can see you take care of
   global events and integrated marketing programs" — booked meetings even with zero discovery
   (PackSafe, 380708810). Weak generic version ("you do marketing, right?") got "No, I'm in
   sales" (379343161).
8. **Situational awareness beats scripts.** "I'm assuming you're driving — should I call back
   later?" built instant rapport on a deal-stage call (DevRev, 373877651). Soft permission
   openers ("Is it a decent time for a quick minute?") kept cold calls alive (380708810).

## 3. What kills (team-level, receipts on file)

- **Pitching through an authority or relevance objection.** 22 authority + 22 relevance
  objections in the May corpus; in NO call where the SDR kept pitching after one did a meeting
  book. The objection IS the discovery moment: stop, one question, referral pivot.
- **Referral agreed but never captured.** "Anyone you can tell me?" → "Yes" → hangup with no
  name (379343161); "willing to find a departmental contact" accepted vaguely (Epsilon,
  378424295). A verbal yes with no name/channel converts at ~zero.
- **Ignoring timing objections.** "I'm heading towards the meeting" at 7s → 74-second scripted
  monologue → Not Interested that was really a timing rejection (380235133). Correct move
  observed converting: acknowledge, name a specific callback slot, end (381657773 got "call
  back tomorrow" in 63s by doing half of this).
- **Continuing after "not interested" / "remove me".** Worst case on tape: 5 explicit
  data-deletion requests ignored across 75+ seconds, then the SDR read the prospect's email
  address back after they denied having it (Parafin, 379431193 — compliance flag). First
  removal request = acknowledge, confirm removal, end. Always.
- **Stale/wrong-contact dials.** A contact called 2.5 years after leaving the company
  (ExtraHop, 379457144 — now at GuidePoint); two retirees (380272308, 381577380); a wrong
  number demanding list removal (379459249). Data freshness is a conversion factor AND a
  compliance factor — verify current employment before any touch.
- **Meetings booked with zero discovery** (PackSafe 380708810, PTx 377354301: no value prop, no
  agenda). The booking metric overstates quality; the pre-meeting email must then carry the
  entire why (one discovery question + agenda), or the meeting no-shows/dies.
- **Unanswered identity challenges.** "What is Unbound?" / "What is this regarding?" answered
  with more script instead of one plain sentence (380708810, 379297424). Have the one-liner
  ready: a B2B brand-and-demand agency — who we work with, what outcome.
- **Self-undercutting.** "You won't listen to my weird accent" (380235133); audibly doubting
  the pitch mid-delivery (Prophecy, 378978947). If the fit feels wrong, ask — don't disclaim.

## 4. How prospects actually talk — objection taxonomy + voice of customer

Frequency in the May corpus (56 substantive calls): timing 39 · authority 22 · relevance 22 ·
email-deflection dominates "other" 21. Deflections to expect, verbatim: "send me an email" ·
"what is this regarding?" · "who is this?" · "not a good time" · "text me what the call's
regarding" (381211815) · "can you message me on LinkedIn?" (380255356).

Pain language worth reusing in drafts (their words, not marketing-speak):
- Registrations vs titles: "for the most part we're able to... not always, and also looking at
  the job titles... CSOs or director-plus, and that is our kind of ICP to get" (374832207).
- Post-event dropoff (confirmed unprompted): "the events you guys are running drive great
  engagement, but once the event wraps up, the follow-up journey falls apart" (372595608).
- Outcome over inputs: "one, two people, that is your concern — my concern is outcome, can you
  fix 10 meetings in a month... if you don't get a meeting for 10, what will you do?" (377720687).
- Vendor-that-feels-internal: previous SDR team "operated in separate systems with no
  visibility into call data"; what they want is "external, but really embedded within our
  team" (379100033).
- Proof-first buying: "I'm not wanting to commit to meetings until I see audience alignment
  first" (374861336); "it depends on what you offer and what your service is about —
  summarize it and send it by email" (378206266).
- Budget/org reality: "we had a little bit of our budget cut due to [the incoming VP's] plan...
  we just don't know yet" (373877651); "no additional budget for the next quarter" (378613943);
  "we have a demand gen team based in the US... it wouldn't fall in my remit, I wouldn't have
  the authority" (381111513); "changes should go through our UK or US division" (378169083);
  "I'm very junior... I'm not the one who has any budget" (372621696).

## 5. Drafting implications — emails (adds to context\copy-rules.md, does not replace it)

- **The email must stand alone.** Prospects routinely never saw prior collateral ("I cannot
  open it now because I'm driving", 377354301). Assume zero prior context every time.
- **One concrete capability, not a services tour** — prospects explicitly ask for this
  (LeverX). Pick the single capability the trigger points at; drop the rest.
- **Numbers that survive a lookup.** Only §1 approved figures. A prospect class that says "my
  concern is outcome" and benchmarks ROI will check.
- **Give the email a job**: either it delivers the promised artifact (audience count, focused
  summary — pattern §2.3), or it names what CANNOT travel by email (ICP data → the meeting
  reason — pattern §2.2). An email that does neither is the "I'll send info" death path
  (9 of 18 Asked-for-Info calls ended with no next-contact anchor).
- **Buying committee is real at every size**: CMO above the director (Quantexa); three champions
  + SOW still frozen by an incoming VP (DevRev); junior contact with no budget but referral
  power (Info-Tech). Drafts address the champion AND arm them for the approver.
- **New-exec two-sidedness** (timing intel): a leader 0-90 days in role is building their
  program = high-intent window; an exec ARRIVING above an in-flight deal freezes it ("budget
  cut due to his incoming plan"). Both belong in why-now framing.
- Channel respect: personal-cell contact poisoned an otherwise-fit prospect (CDW, 374524878) —
  repair before pitch; work channels only.

## 6. SDR call-pointer implications (what goes in every play's talk-track section)

1. 5-second identity confirmation before ANY pitch material (wrong-person discovered at 83s of
   pitch, 377283224).
2. One-sentence "what is Unbound" answer ready for the identity challenge.
3. Open = the account's trigger (why you, why now) + ONE discovery question. Never the
  AWS monologue first — pitch-before-discovery was the team's dominant pattern (8/15 Kumar,
  9/18 Saylee) and correlates with flat outcomes.
4. At any role-reveal or authority objection: stop, ask who owns it, CAPTURE the name/channel
   before hangup.
5. Email-deflection counter loaded: the "ICP data can't travel by email" reason + a named
   callback slot as fallback.
6. Close = logistics locked while on the line (invite sent + verbally confirmed). "I'll follow
   up next month" with no anchor is a lost deal politely postponed (19 of 56 calls).
7. Timing objection = specific callback offer, end call. Nothing else.
8. First "not interested"/"remove me" = acknowledge, at most one soft exit question, out.
9. Banned phrases per §1. Every claim from the play's evidence, nothing else.
