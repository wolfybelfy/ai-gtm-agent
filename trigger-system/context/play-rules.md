# Play Rules

## Evidence grades

Every business-pain statement must carry exactly one internal grade:

- `observed`: directly supported by fresh company-specific evidence.
- `corpus_supported_hypothesis`: supported by the field corpus and connected to a verified company trigger, but not confirmed for this company.
- `unknown`: evidence is insufficient; do not state the pain in prospect copy.

Facts remain facts and hypotheses remain hypotheses. “No evidence found” always includes the source coverage limitation.

## Build order

1. Confirm the account is not suppressed.
2. Confirm a STRIKE verdict and read its evidence ledger.
3. Describe what the change may create or break for the affected team.
4. Select one capability from the commercial truth register.
5. Infer the dynamic buying committee without a numeric target.
6. Choose a smaller outreach subset and explain why each person is included.
7. Verify current employer, role, work location, and work contact data through ZoomInfo.
8. Draft one initial email for each eligible selected contact.

## Committee and contact rules

Champion, decision-maker, influencer, blocker, connector, and insider are optional functional labels. Never force a role into the committee to complete a pattern. Committee membership does not automatically make someone an outreach target.

Only contacts verified as currently employed by the account and based in the United States can receive a v1 draft. Non-US contacts remain research-only holds. Missing or ambiguous location is a hold, not an assumption.

## Email rules

- Plain text, normally 60-90 words and 3-5 sentences.
- A short, lowercase subject, normally 1-3 words and without punctuation.
- Context followed by implication; do not recap the source mechanically.
- One capability and no service tour.
- At most one proof figure, matched to the use case and currently approved.
- A low-friction, forwardable question. No first-touch meeting request.
- No surveillance language, fake familiarity, congratulations filler, guilt bumps, “thoughts?”, em dashes, AI-shaped symmetry, or invented URLs.
- Never mention filing mechanics, requisition IDs, scoring, monitoring, or the research system.
- Never promise a one-pager, audit, sample, plan, benchmark, or other artifact unless it already exists and is explicitly approved for this play.
- Never generate a follow-up sequence in v1.

## Claim rules

`config/commercial-truth.json` is authoritative. The latest explicit ruling wins. Omit a claim when its status is not approved, its review date has passed, its source is unavailable, its client identity is not cleared, or its use case does not match.

Blocked categories are not softened into a near-equivalent. If no proof survives, write a useful email without proof.

## Output contract

The AI writes a candidate play for deterministic validation. It must include the evidence receipt, why-now, pain statement and grade, chosen capability, committee reasoning, outreach subset, draft subject/body, proof ID or null, conflicts, holds, and omitted claims. The validator, not the model, decides whether a draft payload is eligible for Outlook.
