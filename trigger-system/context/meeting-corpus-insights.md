## 4. What worked in the meeting (receipts in the transcripts)

1. **The audience-alignment precondition, honored, converts to a champion.** The benepass
   prospect states ON TAPE why the meeting happened: "I usually require audience, like ICP
   alignment before I even schedule a meeting... that's why I canceled it, but I'm glad you
   called me and made sure that I joined." (Saylee recovered a last-minute cancel by phone.)
   By the end the prospect volunteers the target-account list and says the heat-map layer
   would make them "more of a champion and an advocate". This is cold-call corpus pattern
   §2.3 completing its arc in a real meeting.
2. **Discovery-first beats deck-first at the meeting stage too.** Georgia ran ~30 min on
   kyriba almost deck-free ("I haven't managed to tell you too much about us") and the
   prospect handed over everything: partner targets (8 this year, 2 signed), ARR target
   ($500K EMEA, ~$600K US colleague), deal sizes ($60–70K avg direct, $100K first partner
   deal), budget sources (CRO + CMO funds; champion is not the budget holder), enablement
   gaps (level-2 discovery + level-3 pre-sales "under construction"), IPP screengrabbed with
   permission, next 45-min meeting locked with date+time before hangup, data snapshot
   promised BEFORE that meeting. This is the model discovery call.
3. **Mispositioning recovered by reframe + relevant client proof.** The ptc prospect opened
   with "I expected something different... it's really content syndication, right?" and "my
   management don't believe anymore in the MQL, SQL out of content syndication". Ritchie
   recovered: full-stack reframe, BANT walk-through with the budget/authority/timing/need
   gates spelled out, pay-for-performance + replacement guarantee, and a same-vertical client
   (Jato Dynamics, automotive) the prospect could look up live — ended with a locked proposal
   meeting. Lesson: prospects arrive from SDR outreach expecting "a platform" — the exact
   misperception the cold-call corpus flagged — and the seller must reframe early.
4. **Insider personalization opens doors.** David (cardless) knew the prospect's ex-employer
   (Capital One) AND named the people Unbound works with there; the prospect knew them.
   The same call: "something stood out to me [in the cold email], so I figured I'd meet you"
   — from a prospect who says they never take cold meetings. (lumen: the "do you know Phil
   Pearman" move built rapport even on a miss.)
5. **Deliver the data count IN the deck.** Ilda (benepass) presented audience + account
   counts per segment (with the prospect's own 3-bucket segmentation) — the prospect engaged
   deeply, corrected the keyword logic ("compensation AND benefits together only"), and asked
   for more layers. The artifact does the selling.
6. **Ask for the TAL under NDA.** Ritchie (ptc): "I'm happy to sign an NDA, but could you
   send me your target account list?" → prospect offered a partial list; fallback = run the
   ICP and bring a sample list ("you don't need to share it with me"). Derek (cardless) same
   ask → sample agreed.
7. **Referral routing when the buyer isn't in the room.** lumen: discovery surfaced that
   Partner Engagement Managers control third-party budgets, got a named demand-gen contact
   via LinkedIn + an offer to circulate materials + the prospect volunteering to ask PEMs
   about third-party usage. A no-fit call converted into org-map + referral.
8. (scc 2026-08-19) **The flipped-funnel narrative lands as a learning story, not a pitch.**
   Ram tells it as a confession: programmatic through Unbound's media site "was getting OUR
   brand popular within those accounts and not necessarily the client... a client is simply a
   logo on a landing page" - so phases 1-2 now run on the CLIENT website (their content,
   their socials, their pixel data), and Unbound's media site only enters at lead-gen where
   third-party independence converts better. The prospect's summary back ("so it's a full
   funnel campaign then") shows it landed.
9. (scc 2026-08-19) **The diagnostic question + free audit as the wedge**: "you know your
   ICP, but does your ICP know you?... I can do a simple website audit and tell them what
   percentage of their traffic is coming from their ICP." Root-cause framing sold with it:
   syndication leads die because the second touch lands 4-6 weeks later (brand-recall gap).
10. (scc 2026-08-19) **Channel arithmetic as the "secret sauce"**: programmatic ~1 click per
   1,000 impressions vs email 60-80 clicks per 1,000 sends -> email (own database) drives
   the FIRST traffic, programmatic retargets the captured cookie audience. Also the ABM
   traffic-sources frame (LinkedIn / programmatic / outbound-email publishers) and the
   Meta/Google unlock via client-site pixels once audience thresholds are met (Ram's
   threshold garbled on tape: "a thousand users or twelve thousand two hundred" - unverified).
11. (scc 2026-08-19) **Work-backwards lead processing + always-on air cover (MeridianLink
   pattern)**: ask "how do you process leads?" FIRST, then design backwards - Unbound email
   touch 1, Unbound BDR touch 2 (verify intent/title, offer second content), then client SDR;
   accounts with leads go into an always-on programmatic nurture so "while you're pursuing
   [2-3] leads... it's also educating the other buyers" (20+ member enterprise committees).
   Same educate-the-committee logic as the trigger-system's own air-cover play.
12. (scc 2026-08-19) **The recording-as-artifact close**: on a hard stop, Ram offers the
   pitch recording for the prospect's four colleagues plus a 45-min group re-pitch - turning
   a cut-short call into an internal-circulation asset and a committee meeting.

## 5. What hurt or nearly hurt (receipts)

- **Inconsistent numbers between sellers** (§2). A prospect who hears Ritchie's "1M
  subscribers" and David's "3M community" in the same buying process has a reason to doubt
  both. Any two Unbound people on the same account must use one set.
- **Long company monologue**: David's cardless intro ran ~10 minutes before the prospect's
  problem was explored (the prospect stayed engaged, but the cold-call corpus says this is
  the risk pattern; Ritchie's ptc open also front-loaded ~3.5 min of company stats).
- **Not knowing BANT while selling BANT**: Ilda (harness): "I always forget what BANT is
  too" — Saylee rescued with a clean definition. Ritchie's fluent BANT walk-throughs
  (ptc/avvale) are the reference version. (avvale prospect didn't know the term either —
  explaining it landed well; the term itself is jargon to marketers.)
- **Wrong-name slip**: Derek addressed the cardless prospect by a completely different name
  once mid-call (the prospect let it pass). Pre-meeting internal chatter (other clients'
  business, scheduling complaints) is also being captured on transcripts before the prospect
  joins — cardless + kyriba both. Transcription-on-join = everything is on the record.
- **Thread death by rep departure**: ptc's prior Unbound thread (a different PTC marketer)
  died when the Unbound rep ("John") left. The new thread only exists because an SDR
  re-approached a different person. Continuity on handover is a real revenue leak.
- **Meeting booked on a misunderstanding** (ptc, §4.3): recoverable, but only because the
  seller could reframe; the SDR recording the prospect requested pre-call was never sent
  ("I requested to listen to the recording, but I haven't received that one yet").
- (scc 2026-08-19) **The signals ask went unanswered on tape**: the prospect asked directly
  for "contract renewals coming up... recent jobs won" intent data; Ram answered honestly no
  (partner sources "limited and... highly targeted with heavy budgets") and pivoted to "we
  probably leverage AI to build agents that can give us notification on certain changes...
  leading indicators" - "there's certainly some brainstorming we need to do. I don't have an
  exact answer for you." A live client asked for exactly the capability class this repo runs
  internally. See section 8 flag.
- (scc 2026-08-19) Client-name discipline on tape: the flagship case-study client's name is
  ambiguous audio ("Toto"/"Totale") on a recording the prospect was invited to circulate
  internally - and the numbers cited drift slightly from the approved sheet (section 2).
