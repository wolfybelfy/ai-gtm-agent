## 4. How to combine with the call corpus (drafting/talk-track guidance)

- **Invert to question-first on cold calls.** These openers are proof-then-question; the corpus
  (§2.1, §6.3) shows discovery-before-pitch converts and pitch-first correlates with flat
  outcomes. On a cold call: open with the account's trigger + the persona's discovery question
  from §2; hold the case-study figure for the follow-up — ideally as the curiosity hook
  ("the case study includes the ICP data we used — that can't travel by email", corpus §2.2).
- **In emails**, the persona table gives the one-capability angle per title (corpus §5: one
  concrete capability, not a services tour); the discovery question doubles as the email's
  closing question.
- **The §1 one-liners are now the standard identity-challenge answers** — corpus §6.2 required
  one and the team never had it on tape.
- **Persona→pitch mapping slots into account maps**: when a play targets a person, pick the
  opener row by their title; the account's trigger supplies the why-now the doc's openers lack.
- Figures: internal prep only until approved (§0). Never recite more than ONE figure per touch
  (corpus §2.2: stat recitation was flat or harmful every time).
