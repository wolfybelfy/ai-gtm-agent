# Copy rules — for a buyer who writes this copy for a living

## Structure (every first-touch email)
1. **Cell-specific fact** (from a signal with a URL). Never a compliment. Never "congrats."
2. **Perspective**: what typically breaks in exactly this situation (from pain_hypothesis) — one sentence, stated as pattern, not accusation.
3. **Peer proof**: name the relevant peer (Aqua Security / Bugcrowd / Rubrik / AWS OpenSearch / Todyl). No invented metrics — we publish none.
4. **Soft CTA**: offer the artifact, not the meeting. "Want the pre-RSA pipeline framework we use? No call needed." The artifact is on a tracked link; engagement = the real intent signal, and it starts the speed-to-lead clock.
5. **Lateral reference overrides all of the above when it exists**: open with "We work with your <sibling cell> team." It converts cold email into internal referral.

## Multi-threading
- 3–5 contacts per cell, staggered ≥48h, **different angle per role** (champion: capacity/execution · economic: pipeline/cost-per-opp · influencer: stack/calendar fit). Never the same email to five people at one company.
- Cadence per contact: Day 0 email → Day 2 LinkedIn connect (no note) → Day 5 email 2 (NEW evidence, never a bump) → Day 9 call (SDR, armed with the brief) → Day 14 breakup (light, permissioned exit).
- 3 subject-line variants per campaign, 3–4 words, internal-colleague tone. Deliverability + real A/B read.

## Humanizer (strip before anything is exported — automate as a formula/pass)
- Kill: em-dashes, "delve," "in today's landscape," "I hope this finds you well," bolded summary lines, tri-colon rhythm ("X, Y, and Z" stacked), bullet lists in emails, exclamation marks.
- One idea per sentence. Under 90 words for first touch. Reads like a colleague's Slack message, not a newsletter.
- People tolerate AI-assisted outreach; they do not tolerate carelessly formatted AI outreach — the formatting says you didn't read it either.

## Never
- Biographical personalization (alma mater, hometown, hobbies) — signals a tool, not a human, to this buyer.
- Metrics we can't source in writing. Fabricated urgency. "Quick question" subject lines. Asking for 15/30 minutes in touch one.

## Gates
- Tier 1 (priority accounts + existing-logo siblings): a human reviews EVERY send. The minute saved is not worth the logo.
- Tier 2/3: automatable AFTER the pilot proves precision, within limits.yaml volume caps.
