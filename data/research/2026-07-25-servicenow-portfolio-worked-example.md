# Worked Example — ServiceNow "Autonomous Security & Risk" Portfolio
### The conversation-signal strategy, run on real captured evidence (2026-07-25)

> **How to read the evidence labels** (this is the whole discipline of the doc):
> - **[OBSERVED]** — captured live and quoted verbatim. Real.
> - **[VERIFIED]** — a corporate fact confirmed against a live, HTTP-200 source this session (linked in the appendix).
> - **[HYPOTHESIS]** — an inference we are allowed to make *and must label*. Not fact.
> - **[ILLUSTRATIVE]** — a placeholder for something unknowable from outside (CRM state, a person's private buying intent, exact drafts, sales outcome). Never presented as real.
>
> Capture method: authenticated LinkedIn read through the isolated WSL sandbox (burner account, no host cookies). No person's words were invented; where a signal did not exist, this doc says so.

## 0. The "big logo trap" — why we do NOT target ServiceNow the company

Fit for **whole-company ServiceNow = LOW.** ServiceNow runs one of the largest in-house B2B marketing machines in software; it does not buy demand-generation from an agency at the parent level.

This is not an assumption — it is what the live capture showed. Two real ServiceNow marketing directors:

No operating-pain disclosure.
- **[OBSERVED]** *Vivek Khandelwal — Sr. Marketing Director, APAC (ABM / B2B growth).* Own posts are 7–9 years old; recent activity is two congratulatory comments. No disclosure.

**Decision:** route the parent entity to *market-insight only*. **Do not** hand ServiceNow-the-logo to an SDR. Resolve the account into a *winnable buying center* instead.

## 4. Clay's job (six stages, grounded)

1. **Receive** — the post, the quoted comment, and the profile data (Lizi's real record). **[OBSERVED input]**
2. **Resolve** — company (Armis; pending ServiceNow) + current role (VP Rev & Growth Mktg). **[OBSERVED]**
3. **CRM lookup** — HubSpot: existing record / owner / history *before* creating. **[ILLUSTRATIVE state]**
4. **Enrich** — work-email + phone waterfall; account context (the three deals, the portfolio); build the buying group: VP Revenue/Growth Marketing + demand-gen director + product-marketing lead for the portfolio + RevOps/marketing-ops owner. **[ILLUSTRATIVE contact values]**
5. **Brief** — assemble the SDR-ready record + suggested channel copy (§5).
6. **Upsert + route** — write back to HubSpot and route the approved lead to the sequencer (Outreach / Salesloft are documented examples). Human review before any sequencer handoff.

Clay is the data & orchestration layer — it does **not** replace the SDR's accept/reject judgement.

## 6. SDR cadence (pilot design — not a benchmark)

1. Day 1 — personalized email + first call
2. Day 3 — LinkedIn connection / relevant engagement
3. Day 5 — second call + short voicemail
4. Day 7 — proof-led email (AWS OpenSearch)
5. Day 10 — account-level follow-up or a carefully chosen second stakeholder (demand-gen director / RevOps)
6. Day 12 — final problem-focused email + call
7. After — qualify, refer, nurture, monitor, or close

Every touch has a distinct job; the sequence never re-sends the same Calendly link without adding new value.

---

## 7. Qualify → handoff → disposition

A reply is not an opportunity. The SDR confirms: is the issue current? is there an active initiative? does she own/influence it? what's the current approach? another stakeholder? deeper conversation justified? The account director takes over after a qualified meeting. Every record gets a HubSpot disposition (opportunity / nurture-with-date / referred / no-initiative / wrong-fit / no-response).

---

## 8. Pilot measurement (yield-first, no invented forecasts)

First two weeks measure *discovery yield* before any outreach quota. Then: useful signals per 100 substantive items reviewed · target-account matches · Clay enrichment coverage · SDR acceptance · signal→first-touch time · calls connected · positive replies · meetings held · qualified opportunities · nurture/rejection reasons · performance by theme/proof/channel. **No reply-rate, meeting-rate, or pipeline forecast is invented.**

---

## What the live run actually proved (findings)

1. **The buying-center resolution is correct, and the data proves it.** The strongest real signal came from an **acquired-company** leader (Armis / Lizi), *not* the parent — exactly as the "big logo trap" logic predicted. ServiceNow's own directors post only promo.
2. **Enterprise marketing leaders rarely post raw pain.** Of five leaders captured, one (Lizi) gave an explicit operating-friction disclosure — and even that was retrospective (prior employer). The layer must treat these as *worldview/priority* signals and lean on **comments** and **context** (acquisitions, hiring, event motion) more than on confessional posts.
3. **Coverage is uneven and must be stated, not hidden.** Traceloop has no marketing surface; the parent's is noise. Honest gaps are part of the output.
