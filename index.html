Working strategy 23 July 2026

VP Marketing / VP Strategy working discussion

Using LinkedIn conversations to give outbound a better reason to start.

I came up with a way to find senior B2B marketers discussing problems that may be relevant to their work, validate whether the person and account fit Unbound IA, and give an SDR enough context to work the account properly.

A comment can reveal a problem.

Clay can turn the signal into a usable account record.

An SDR still has to create the conversation over several touches.

01

The commercial premise

A comment is a signal, not a converted lead.

Normal ICP data can tell us that a company is the right size, industry and revenue band. It can tell us that a person works in demand generation, ABM, revenue marketing or marketing operations. It cannot tell us what is occupying that person's attention today.

A substantive comment may add that missing context. Someone might explain that lead volume is not becoming pipeline, attribution is not trusted, sales follow-up is inconsistent, or targets have increased while resources have not.

That does not mean they are shopping for an agency. It gives us a better hypothesis to investigate and a more relevant starting point if the account is already worth pursuing.

Working rule Clay prepares the record. The SDR works the account.

The strategy succeeds only when the signal moves through account validation, CRM checks, human judgement, multi-channel follow-up and sales qualification.

02

Start to finish

The complete operating model.

This is one continuous process. Each step earns the right to move to the next.

  1. 01

    Research

    Find a relevant B2B marketing discussion

    Search by problem theme. Prefer posts with enough engagement to produce substantive comments.

    Output: post URL and reaction count

  2. 02

    Research

    Capture comments with real business language

    Keep first-person statements about current work, constraints, changes or commercial consequences. Ignore generic agreement.

    Output: exact comment, profile URL and problem theme

  3. 03

    Clay + reviewer

    Resolve the current company and role

    Match the profile to the present employer. Check the company against the target-account list before deeper enrichment.

    Output: account fit and buyer relevance

  4. 04

    CRM / account owner

    Check CRM ownership and history

    Look for an existing contact, account owner, previous outreach, open opportunity, customer relationship or a reason another team should handle the account.

    Output: route, reject or continue

  5. 05

    Clay

    Enrich only the records that passed

    Find and validate work email and phone, confirm seniority, add company context and identify nearby buying roles.

    Output: complete contact and account record

  6. 06

    SDR + account owner

    Accept or reject the SDR-ready brief

    The SDR sees the evidence, problem hypothesis, proof that fits, first email and call opener. Weak records return to research.

    Output: accepted lead with a named owner

  7. 07

    SDR

    Work several channels

    Use email, calls and a relevant LinkedIn touch. Each touch adds something new instead of repeating the same request.

    Output: response, referral, no response or rejection

  8. 08

    SDR

    Qualify the response

    Confirm whether the issue is current, who owns it, what the team is doing now and whether a deeper conversation makes sense.

    Output: qualified meeting or defined nurture

  9. 09

    Account director

    Take over the qualified conversation

    The account director runs discovery with the context already collected. An opportunity is created only when there is a real initiative and next step.

    Output: qualified opportunity, nurture or close

  10. 10

    RevOps + strategy

    Feed the result back

    Record which problem, evidence, proof, channel and role produced movement. Rejection reasons matter as much as meetings.

    Output: better account and signal decisions next time

03

Finding useful discussions

Search for the problem before searching for the person.

A

Use reaction count to decide what to review

Start with posts showing 40+ reactions in the Google result because they are more likely to contain a useful comment set. Prioritize 100+ reactions when available. The number does not prove comment quality; it helps decide where to spend review time.

B

Search narrow problem language

Use phrases a practitioner would write when discussing pipeline, contribution, ABM progression, sales follow-up, budget pressure or marketing operations.

C

Build a creator watchlist from yield

Keep authors whose comment sections repeatedly attract senior buyers and real operating discussion. Follower count is less useful than target-account and substantive-comment yield.

D

Run small searches several times a day

The spare PC can run scheduled discovery and pass selected post data into the review queue. The pilot should measure actual yield before the number of searches expands.

Practical starting query

Post URL, author, date, reaction count, problem theme and a short note on why the discussion is worth opening. No contact is enriched at this point.

The supplied evidence

What these comments support, and what they do not.

Evidence decision

05

Before enrichment

Four checks decide whether the signal becomes an account lead.

  1. 1

    Is the current company on the target-account list?

    Resolve the company first. If it is not an approved account, retain the comment as market insight and stop the contact path.

    Continue only when the account fits.
  2. 2

    Does the person own or influence this problem?

    Check the actual remit, not only seniority. A demand generation leader may own pipeline creation. A marketing operations leader may own measurement. A general CMO may sponsor both.

    Continue only when the role fits the issue.
  3. 3

    Does CRM history change the route?

    Check CRM ownership, prior emails, meetings, open opportunities, customers, partners and closed-lost history. Existing relationships go to the named owner.

    Continue only with a clear owner and no conflicting motion.
  4. 4

    Is there enough evidence to justify an SDR's time?

    Look for one supporting signal such as relevant hiring, a new marketing leader, active campaign work, a recent post on the same theme or previous first-party engagement.

    If the comment stands alone, keep monitoring.

This is where the strategy protects SDR capacity. A clever comment from the wrong account never becomes a task. A perfect account with a weak comment stays in the normal account programme.

06

Where Clay fits

Clay turns an approved signal into a usable sales record.

Clay is the enrichment and routing layer. The CRM remains the record of ownership and commercial history. The SDR remains responsible for the conversation.

  1. 01

    Input

    Receive the selected comment

    The local collector sends JSON through a Clay webhook, or the reviewer uploads a small CSV during the manual pilot.

    Post URL, comment, profile URL, date, reactions and problem theme

  2. 02

    Identity

    Resolve person, company and role

    Match the profile to the current company domain and verify the title before any outreach details are generated.

    Current employer, role, seniority and department

  3. 03

    CRM

    Look up before creating

    Check Salesforce, HubSpot or the current CRM for the person, account, owner, open opportunity and prior contact. Duplicate records do not enter a new sequence.

    CRM route and ownership decision

  4. 04

    Enrichment

    Find verified contact details and account context

    Use waterfalls to find a work email and phone. Add company size, industry, region, hiring and other context that can change the relevance judgement.

    Validated work email and phone plus account context

  5. 05

    Preparation

    Build the SDR-ready brief

    Summarize the observed signal, what remains a hypothesis, which proof fits, the call opener and the next task. A human review approves, edits or rejects it.

    Brief that can be acted on without new research

  6. 06

    Routing

    Send the approved record into the working cadence

    Upsert the CRM and route to Salesloft, Outreach or the sales-engagement tool already used by the SDR team.

    Named SDR, first task and complete source context

Verified capability

Clay documents webhook intake, work-email waterfalls, CRM lookups and direct actions for adding people to Salesloft cadences or Outreach sequences. Its outbound workflow also includes a human review step before handoff.

08

Pilot outreach motion

One signal becomes several useful touches.

This is a proposed pilot cadence, not a fixed benchmark. Timing and channel mix should be adjusted when Unbound IA has its own connect, reply and meeting data.

  1. Day 1
    Email + call

    Introduce the problem, then test the hypothesis

    Send the short personalized email. Call the same day while the research is current. The call asks a useful question; it does not recite the email.

    Purpose: earn recognition and learn whether the topic is real.

  2. Day 3
    LinkedIn touch

    Create a second point of recognition

    View the profile, connect where appropriate or engage with a relevant current post. Do not paste the email into a connection request.

    Purpose: make the SDR familiar without forcing another pitch.

  3. Day 5
    Call + voicemail

    Use the commercial consequence

    Ask how the team shows which marketing activity helped pipeline progress. Leave a short voicemail only if there is a clear reason to call back.

    Purpose: test relevance in the fastest channel.

  4. Day 7
    Proof email

    Add evidence instead of repeating the first message

    Share the most relevant published Unbound IA example. Explain the operating approach it proves and keep the claims inside the case study.

    Purpose: show that Unbound IA has done related work.

  5. Day 10
    Account decision

    Choose whether to multi-thread

    If account evidence is strong and the first contact is not the clear owner, the SDR may approach one adjacent stakeholder with a role-specific angle. Do not send the same copy to the whole team.

    Purpose: find the right owner, not create volume.

  6. Day 12
    Email + call

    Close the first sequence cleanly

    Offer one defined conversation and make it easy to say that the timing is wrong. If there is no response, stop the sequence.

    Purpose: get a decision or release the SDR's time.

  7. After
    CRM disposition

    Qualify, refer, nurture, monitor or close

    A new signal can reopen the account later. Silence alone is not a reason to continue sending.

    Purpose: preserve account history and improve the next decision.

Research basis

Gong recommends a multi-channel cadence and treating six to twelve touches as a starting range, then adjusting the sequence from the team's own history. The cadence above is intentionally smaller and designed for a controlled pilot.

09

After somebody responds

A positive reply is the start of qualification.

  1. 1

    Confirm the issue

    Is this a current operating problem, an old experience, or simply a point of view? The SDR replaces the hypothesis with the buyer's actual words.

  2. 2

    Understand ownership

    Who is responsible for the outcome? Who is affected by it? Which marketing, sales, operations or finance stakeholders influence the decision?

  3. 3

    Understand the current approach

    What has the team tried? Where does the existing process break? Is the gap strategic, operational, creative, channel-related or measurement-related?

  4. 4

    Decide the next commercial step

    If there is a current initiative and relevant owner, book a focused meeting with the account director. If timing is later, set a nurture date and the next useful touch.

Qualified

Meeting held and a real initiative confirmed

The account director owns discovery. Create a qualified opportunity only when there is a defined problem, relevant stakeholders and agreed next step.

Not ready

Relevant issue, no current project

Move to nurture with a date, content or market trigger that justifies the next contact.

Referral

Right account, wrong person

Ask for the correct owner or use Clay to map the adjacent role. Preserve the context in CRM.

Closed

No fit, no issue or no response

Record the reason. Do not call it a failed opportunity if it never became one.

The email tests interest. It does not tell Maya that a LinkedIn comment triggered the message, and it does not push a calendar link before she has shown interest.

Illustrative call opener / day 1

"Maya, Sam from Unbound IA. I sent you a note because a lot of enterprise marketing teams can report where pipeline started but still struggle to show what moved it after creation. I wanted to ask how Northstar separates those two today."

If she says the issue is not relevant, the SDR learns quickly and closes the hypothesis. If she engages, the SDR asks how the current process works and who else is involved.

Subject: source vs contribution

Hi Maya,

One practical example behind my note: for AWS OpenSearch, the programme connected paid and organic promotion, content, events, retargeting, nurture and SDR follow-up before launch.

The published results included about 3x live attendance versus the prior baseline, 55% net-new target accounts and more than 40% post-event engagement.

If Maya replies positively

The SDR qualifies the current initiative and then offers a focused contribution and pipeline-progression discussion. The Calendly link can be used at this point to remove scheduling friction.

If Maya refers Marketing Ops

Route the context to the referred owner. Do not restart with generic outreach or contact the entire department.

If nobody responds

Stop after the agreed sequence, record no response, and monitor for a second signal. Reopen only with new information.

12

How to test this without pretending we know the yield

Measure discovery first. Set volume second.

  1. Weeks 1-2

    Discovery-only calibration

    Choose a small named-account panel and one or two problem themes. Review posts and comments manually. Measure how often a substantive comment resolves to a target account and relevant role.

    No outreach quota is set yet because the useful-signal yield is still unknown.

  2. Week 3

    Clay and CRM setup

    Build the webhook or CSV intake, company-first filtering, CRM lookup, work email and phone enrichment, SDR brief and dispositions. Run ten test records through the full route.

    The SDR should receive a complete, understandable record without repeating the research.

  3. Weeks 4-6

    Controlled activation

    Activate only records accepted by the SDR and account owner. Use one agreed multi-channel cadence and keep an account-fit-only comparison group.

    Learn whether the additional context improves useful conversations, not merely opens or clicks.

  4. End of week 6

    Operating review

    Review yield, enrichment cost, SDR acceptance, channel activity, replies, meetings, opportunity decisions and rejection reasons. Decide which themes and sources deserve another cycle.

    Scale only the part of the motion that has earned more capacity.

What the VP view should track

Discovery quality
Useful signals per 100 substantive comments reviewed and target-account matches per source.
Operating quality
Clay enrichment coverage, SDR acceptance and time from signal to first touch.
Engagement quality
Calls connected, positive reply, referral, meeting booked and meeting held.
Pipeline quality
Qualified opportunity, nurture decision, rejection reason and eventual account progression.
Learning quality
Performance by problem theme, creator, proof, role and channel.

Pilot choice The cadence, account count and weekly capacity are design choices. The presentation deliberately avoids inventing a reply-rate, meeting-rate or pipeline forecast before the discovery yield is known.

Where your perspective changes the plan

The decisions I would like to work through together.

  1. 01

    Where should this sit?

    Is this best treated as an ABM account-prioritization layer, an SDR sourcing motion or a shared demand and sales intelligence process?

  2. 02

    Which accounts and problems should go first?

    Which named accounts and two problem themes have the strongest connection to Unbound IA's proof and current commercial priorities?

  3. 03

    Who owns acceptance and activation?

    Should the SDR, account director or RevOps owner approve the record before it enters a cadence?

  4. 04

    Which CRM and sales-engagement stack should the process use?

    The Clay route should match the tools already used by the team rather than introduce another place for SDRs to work.

  5. 05

    When should the second stakeholder enter?

    What account evidence is strong enough to justify careful multi-threading without creating duplicate or uncoordinated outreach?

  6. 06

    What result earns another cycle?

    Which combination of SDR acceptance, positive conversations, meetings held and opportunity quality would justify more research capacity?
